California Law FAQ: Lawful Permanent Residents (Green Card Holders)

ਪੰਜਾਬੀ FAQ: ਗ੍ਰੀਨ ਕਾਰਡ ਧਾਰਕ ਕੈਲੀਫੋਰਨੀਆ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਾਨੂੰਨੀ ਦਸਤਾਵੇਜ਼ਾਂ ਬਾਰੇ ਕੀ ਜਾਣਨ?

One of the most common questions at our Gurdwara seminars: what documentation do Lawful Permanent Residents need under California and federal law?

• Lawful Eligibility: Unexpired U.S. Permanent Resident Card (Form I-551) holders are legally eligible under federal & CA law.
• Primary ID + Form I-551: Bring valid CA Driver's License/ID PLUS your physical unexpired Green Card (especially if 'Federal Limits Apply').
• Handgun Proof of Residency: Current DMV vehicle registration, signed lease, or municipal utility bill matching your license address.
• FSC & 10-Day Wait Apply Equally: Complete the 30-question CA FSC exam and exact 240-hour DROS waiting period.
