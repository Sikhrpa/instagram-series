Why You Need a Mylar Space Blanket in Your Range IFAK — Even in Summer

ਗਰਮੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੀ ਖੂਨ ਵਹਿਣ ਕਾਰਨ ਸਰੀਰ ਠੰਢਾ ਪੈ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ — ਐਮਰਜੈਂਸੀ ਸ਼ੀਟ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਰੱਖੋ

Blood loss destroys the body's ability to stay warm. Dropping just 2°F triggers the Trauma Lethal Triad and stops blood from clotting — even on a 95°F California afternoon.

• 1. Hypothermia (<95°F Core): Massive bleeding chills a patient rapidly, even in hot summer air.
• 2. Coagulopathy: Cold blood loses its chemical ability to form life-saving clots.
• 3. Acidosis: Tissue shock lowers blood pH, accelerating life-threatening decline.
• Action: Insulate & Wrap: Slide a mat/jacket under the patient off bare ground and wrap fully in a Mylar foil blanket.
