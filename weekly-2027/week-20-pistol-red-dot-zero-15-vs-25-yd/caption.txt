Zeroing a Pistol Red Dot: 15 Yards vs. 25 Yards

ਪਿਸਤੌਲ ਰੈੱਡ-ਡੌਟ ਜ਼ੀਰੋਇੰਗ — ੧੫ ਗਜ਼ ਬਨਾਮ ੨੫ ਗਜ਼ ਦਾ ਸਹੀ ਫ਼ਰਕ

Zeroing a pistol optic at 15 or 25 yards creates a remarkably flat trajectory across practical target distances — use our free click calculator to dial once.

• 15-Yard Practical Zero: Easy to see groups clearly; impacts stay within ~1" from 5 yards to 35 yards.
• 25-Yard Precision Zero: Tighter angular alignment at distance; stays flat from 3 yards out to 50 yards.
• Remember 1 MOA Scaling: A 1 MOA pistol dot moves 0.15" per click at 15 yd (0.25" per click at 25 yd).
• Plot Hits Automatically: Skip the mental math with sikhrpa.org/zero-optics.html.
