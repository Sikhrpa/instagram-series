Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!

ਚਾਰ ਸਾਹਿਬਜ਼ਾਦਿਆਂ ਅਤੇ ਮਾਤਾ ਗੁਜਰੀ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਲਾਸਾਨੀ ਸ਼ਹਾਦਤ ਨੂੰ ਕੋਟਿ-ਕੋਟਿ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮ

In solemn remembrance of Baba Ajit Singh Ji, Baba Jujhar Singh Ji (Chamkaur Sahib), Baba Zorawar Singh Ji, Baba Fateh Singh Ji, and Mata Gujri Ji (Sirhind / Fatehgarh Sahib).

• Unshakable Moral Courage: Choosing truth, faith, and dignity over submission in the freezing nights of Poh 1704.
• Zafarnama Ethic of Restraint: Strength in Sikh heritage is bound forever to righteousness, humility, and defense of the innocent.
• Protecting Every Generation: Honoring their memory by living responsibly, serving our Sangat, and keeping every child safe.

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).

ਸਰਬੱਤ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ • ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਰਹੋ, ਜ਼ਿੰਮੇਵਾਰ ਬਣੋ! — Stay Safe, Be Responsible!
