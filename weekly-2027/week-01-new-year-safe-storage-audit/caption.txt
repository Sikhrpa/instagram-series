Start 2027 Right: The Annual Home Safe Storage Audit

ਨਵੇਂ ਸਾਲ 2027 ਦੀ ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ — ਆਪਣੇ ਘਰ ਦੀ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਸਟੋਰੇਜ ਦੀ ਜਾਂਚ ਕਰੋ

Kick off the new year by checking every lockbox battery, safe combination, cable lock, and ammunition storage bin in your household.

• Check Lockbox Batteries: Replace AA/9V batteries in biometric & electronic safes every January and verify backup keys.
• Verify CA PC § 25100 Compliance: Confirm zero firearms are stored unlocked in nightstands, closets, or guest areas.
• Separate & Lock Ammunition: Keep ammunition dry, labeled, and secured in a dedicated locked container.
• Refresh Family Safety Rules: Review STOP • Don't Touch • Leave Area • Tell an Adult with children.
