Commemorating the sacred installation of Sri Adi Granth Sahib Ji at Sachkhand Sri Harmandir Sahib in 1604 by Sri Guru Arjan Dev Ji, with Baba Buddha Ji as first Granthi.

• Universal Wisdom for All Humanity: Gurbani unites hymns of the Sikh Gurus and enlightened Bhagats in timeless harmony.
• Truthful Living & Humility: Let wisdom, patience, and compassion guide every word and action in our daily lives.
• Peace & Sarbat da Bhala: Wishing the entire Sangat worldwide a blessed First Parkash Purab.
