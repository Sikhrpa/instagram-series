Firearm Safety Is for Every Member of the Household

ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਸੈਮੀਨਾਰ ਪਰਿਵਾਰਾਂ, ਬੀਬੀਆਂ ਅਤੇ ਨਵੇਂ ਸਿੱਖਣ ਵਾਲਿਆਂ ਲਈ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਹਨ

Even if a family member never plans to shoot, knowing the 4 Universal Rules and how to verify a safe home storage system protects everyone.

• Zero-Intimidation Environment: Patient, structured instruction built for absolute beginners and women (Bibian).
• Shared Household Literacy: Every adult in a home with firearms should understand safe storage & child safety rules.
• Bilingual Punjabi & English: Clear explanations for parents and elders without confusing jargon.
• Free Learning Tools: Start safely at home anytime with sikhrpa.org.

Explore 100% free English & Gurmukhi safety tools anytime at sikhrpa.org (Link in bio).
