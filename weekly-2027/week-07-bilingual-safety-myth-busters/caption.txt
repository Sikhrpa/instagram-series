3 Dangerous Firearm Safety Myths (English & Gurmukhi)

ਗਲਤ ਧਾਰਨਾ: 'ਮੈਗਜ਼ੀਨ ਕੱਢ ਦਿੱਤੀ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਬੰਦੂਕ ਖਾਲੀ ਹੈ।' — ਸੱਚਾਈ: ਚੈਂਬਰ ਦੀ ਖੁਦ ਜਾਂਚ ਕਰੋ!

Preventable negligent discharges almost always begin with a false assumption. Share these three myth-busters with every new learner.

• Myth 1: Magazine Out = Empty: Reality: A live cartridge can remain inside the chamber. Visually & physically inspect.
• Myth 2: Mechanical Safety = Safe: Reality: Safeties are mechanical devices that can fail — never rely on them over muzzle discipline.
• Myth 3: High Shelf = Childproof: Reality: Kids climb. Only a CA DOJ-certified lockbox, safe, or cable lock secures a home.
