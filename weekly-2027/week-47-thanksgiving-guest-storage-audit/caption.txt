Hosting Thanksgiving Family? Lock Every Firearm Before Guests Arrive

ਥੈਂਕਸਗਿਵਿੰਗ 'ਤੇ ਮਹਿਮਾਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਆਉਣ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ੩-ਮਿੰਟ ਦੀ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਸਟੋਰੇਜ ਜਾਂਚ ਕਰੋ

Busy kitchens, visiting cousins, and overnight luggage make holiday weeks the single most important time to verify 100% locked home storage under CA PC § 25100.

• Lock All Bedside & Closet Firearms: Never leave firearms in nightstands or guest-room closets where kids hide and play.
• Secure Safe Keys & Combinations: Keep backup keys locked away from key hooks and bedroom dressers.
• Check Parked Vehicles: Never leave firearms unattended in cars parked on streets or driveways.
• Need a Free CA DOJ Cable Lock?: Email president@sikhrpa.org or attend any free SikhRPA community clinic.
