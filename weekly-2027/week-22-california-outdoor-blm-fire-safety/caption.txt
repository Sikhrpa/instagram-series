Summer Outdoor Shooting in California: Fire Restrictions & Backstops

ਗਰਮੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਊਟਡੋਰ ਰੇਂਜ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ — ਅੱਗ ਦੀਆਂ ਪਾਬੰਦੀਆਂ ਅਤੇ ਪੱਕੇ ਬੈਕਸਟਾਪ ਦੀ ਜਾਂਚ

California enters peak wildfire season every June. Check BLM and National Forest fire orders BEFORE driving out, and pack a shovel, water, and extinguisher.

• Verify BLM / USFS Fire Orders: Seasonal recreational shooting restrictions activate across CA counties every summer.
• Never Shoot Steel in Dry Grass: Sparks from bi-metal jackets or steel targets can ignite dry brush in seconds.
• Confirm Solid Earthen Berm: Never shoot toward skylines, rocky ridges (ricochets), or blind hiking trails.
• Pack Out 100% of Trash & Brass: Leave California public lands cleaner than you found them.
