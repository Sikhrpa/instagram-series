May Is Stop the Bleed Month: 3 Life-Saving Steps Everyone Should Know

ਮਈ 'ਸਟਾਪ ਦ ਬਲੀਡ' ਮਹੀਨਾ — ਖੂਨ ਵਹਿਣਾ ਰੋਕਣ ਦੇ ੩ ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ ਕਦਮ ਸਿੱਖੋ

Severe bleeding is the #1 preventable cause of death after trauma — whether at a shooting range, highway crash, or community event.

• 1. Ensure Safety & Call 911: Call 'CEASE FIRE!' or secure the scene, then dial 911 immediately with exact location.
• 2. Find the Bleeding Source: Cut or move clothing to locate bright red arterial spurting or pooling blood.
• 3. Compress (Pressure / TQ): Apply a CoTCCC tourniquet 2–3" above limb wounds, or pack junctional wounds & hold firm pressure.
• Get Certified Hands-On: Find an official ACS Stop the Bleed® class near you & study free at sikhrpa.org/trauma-safety.html.
