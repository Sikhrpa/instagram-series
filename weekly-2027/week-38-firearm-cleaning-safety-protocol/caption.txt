Safe Firearm Cleaning Protocol: Preventing Administrative Accidents

ਹਥਿਆਰ ਸਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਨਿਯਮ — ਅਸਲੀ ਗੋਲੀਆਂ ਕਮਰੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਹਰ ਰੱਖੋ

A surprisingly large share of home firearm accidents happen during cleaning and reassembly. Follow this strict 5-step safety routine every single time.

• 1. Remove ALL Live Ammo From Room: Never clean a firearm on a table with loaded magazines or loose cartridges nearby.
• 2. Triple-Check Chamber & Magwell: Remove magazine, lock slide open, visually AND physically feel an empty chamber.
• 3. Ventilate & Protect Eyes: Wear safety glasses against spring tension & solvents; work in a well-ventilated area.
• 4. Function Check Safely: Point in a confirmed safe direction during reassembly function checks, then lock immediately.
