50/200-Yard vs. 100-Yard Rifle Zero: Which Should You Choose?

ਕਾਰਬਾਈਨ ਜ਼ੀਰੋਇੰਗ ਤੁਲਨਾ — 50/200 ਗਜ਼ ਬਨਾਮ 100 ਗਜ਼ ਜ਼ੀਰੋ ਦਾ ਫ਼ਰਕ

Understanding where your bullet crosses your optical line of sight eliminates guesswork from close quarters out to 200+ yards.

• 50/200-Yard Practical Zero: Crosses line of sight at 50 yd, rises <2" at ~120 yd, and crosses zero again at 200 yd.
• 100-Yard Precision Zero: Preferred for magnified scopes — trajectory kisses crosshair at 100 yd and drops smoothly after.
• Account for 2.6" Height-Over-Bore: On either zero, close 3–7 yard shots still hit ~1.5"–2.5" below the dot.
