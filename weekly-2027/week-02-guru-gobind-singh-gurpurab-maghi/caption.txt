Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!

ਧੰਨ ਧੰਨ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਦੇ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ਼ ਪੁਰਬ ਅਤੇ ਮਾਘੀ (ਚਾਲੀ ਮੁਕਤੇ) ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ-ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ!

Honoring Dashmesh Pita, founder of the Khalsa and the Sant-Sipahi (Saint-Soldier) ideal, alongside the supreme devotion of the Chali Mukte at Sri Muktsar Sahib. (Observed Jan 5 Nanakshahi / Jan 15 Lunar.)

• Sant-Sipahi Discipline: Spiritual humility (Sant) paired with courageous, disciplined duty to defend justice (Sipahi).
• Chali Mukte (Maghi • Jan 14): Remembering Mai Bhago Ji and the Forty Liberated Ones for selfless loyalty and forgiveness.
• Modern Stewardship: Carrying Guru Ji's legacy through lawful conduct, compassion, and protecting every household.

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).

ਸਰਬੱਤ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ • ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਰਹੋ, ਜ਼ਿੰਮੇਵਾਰ ਬਣੋ! — Stay Safe, Be Responsible!
