Still Carrying a 'Federal Limits Apply' CA License? Know the Rule

ਜੇ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ CA ਲਾਇਸੈਂਸ 'ਤੇ 'Federal Limits Apply' ਲਿਖਿਆ ਹੈ, ਤਾਂ ਪਾਸਪੋਰਟ ਜਾਂ ਗ੍ਰੀਨ ਕਾਰਡ ਨਾਲ ਰੱਖੋ

Check the top-right corner of your California Driver's License: a Golden Bear with a white star means REAL ID. 'FEDERAL LIMITS APPLY' requires certified legal-presence proof.

• REAL ID (Bear + Star): Satisfies federal primary identification requirements at licensed CA dealers and airports.
• Federal Limits Apply: Must be paired with an unexpired U.S. Passport, certified U.S. Birth Certificate, or Green Card (I-551).
• Handgun Residency Still Separate: Even with REAL ID, handgun paperwork still requires matching CA Proof of Residency.
• Interactive Counter Checklist: Verify every document free at sikhrpa.org/buyer-guide.html.
