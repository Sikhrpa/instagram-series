The Golden Rule of Optic Turrets: Arrows Always Move Bullet Impact (POI)

ਓਪਟਿਕ ਡਾਇਲ ਦਾ ਸੁਨਹਿਰੀ ਨਿਯਮ — UP ਅਤੇ R ਤੀਰ ਗੋਲੀ ਦੇ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ (POI) ਨੂੰ ਹਿਲਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ

Forget twisting dials backwards at the bench. Remember one rule: if your 5-shot group center hits LOW and LEFT, dial UP and RIGHT!

• Always Shoot a 3–5 Shot Group: Never turn turrets after a single shot — measure the group centroid (MPI).
• Hitting LOW? Dial UP: Turn top elevation turret in the direction marked 'UP' to raise impacts.
• Hitting LEFT? Dial R (RIGHT): Turn side windage turret toward 'R' to shift impacts right toward center.
• Zero Math Required: Plot your target hits free at sikhrpa.org/zero-optics.html.
