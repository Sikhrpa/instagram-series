Summer Break Is Coming: Double-Check Safe Storage Before Kids Are Home

ਗਰਮੀਆਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਛੁੱਟੀਆਂ — ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਲਈ ਹਰ ਹਥਿਆਰ ਤਾਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖੋ

When school lets out, kids and neighborhood friends spend far more unsupervised hours indoors. Lock every firearm and review the 4 Child Safety Rules.

• 1. STOP (੧. ਰੁਕੋ): Teach children to freeze immediately if they ever encounter an unattended firearm.
• 2. Don't Touch (੨. ਹੱਥ ਨਾ ਲਾਓ): Never touch it or let another friend pick it up.
• 3. Leave Area (੩. ਦੂਰ ਹਟੋ): Walk away from the room or house right away.
• 4. Tell an Adult (੪. ਵੱਡਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਦੱਸੋ): Report it to a parent or trusted adult immediately — and verify locks before playdates!
