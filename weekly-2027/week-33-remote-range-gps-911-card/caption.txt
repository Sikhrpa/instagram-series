Save a Laminated GPS 911 Card in Your Range Bag Before You Drive Out

ਦੂਰ-ਦੁਰਾਡੇ ਰੇਂਜ 'ਤੇ ਜਾਣ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ GPS ਕੋਆਰਡੀਨੇਟ ਅਤੇ ੯੧੧ ਸਕ੍ਰਿਪਟ ਤਿਆਰ ਰੱਖੋ

Cell reception drops fast on California forest roads and BLM ranges. Writing down exact GPS coordinates, gate codes, and the nearest highway marker shaves critical minutes off EMS response.

• Save Latitude & Longitude Offline: Screenshot or write exact decimal GPS coordinates on a weatherproof card.
• Note Gate Code & Helicopter LZ: Include locked gate combinations and flat open clearing notes for medevac.
• Assign a Highway Escort: Always send one range partner to meet the ambulance at the main highway entrance.
• Generate Your Custom Card: Use our free 911 Range Script tool at sikhrpa.org/trauma-safety.html.
