ਹੋਲਾ ਮੁਹੱਲਾ 2027 ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ-ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ — ਅਨੁਸ਼ਾਸਨ, ਨਿਮਰਤਾ ਅਤੇ ਸੇਵਾ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਕ!

Established by Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji at Anandpur Sahib in 1701, Hola Mohalla celebrates physical readiness, Shastar Vidya discipline, spiritual kirtan, and fearless service.

• Discipline Over Bravado: True martial readiness in Sikh tradition is measured by calm control, never recklessness.
• Reverence for Shastar: Careful maintenance, safe handling, and locked storage honor centuries of Shastar reverence.
• Community & Seva: Gatka, kirtan, and Langar unite generations in strength and compassion.

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).
