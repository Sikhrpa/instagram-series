Pickup Day Ready: Mastering California's Safe Handling Demonstration

ਕੈਲੀਫੋਰਨੀਆ ਸੇਫ ਹੈਂਡਲਿੰਗ ਡੈਮੋ (SHD) — ਡੀਲਰ ਕੋਲ ਪਿਕਅੱਪ ਵਾਲੇ ਦਿਨ ਦੇ ੫ ਕਦਮ

On Day 10 when you return to pick up your firearm, California requires a physical Safe Handling Demonstration using inert dummy rounds. Know the steps ahead of time.

• 1. Safe Muzzle & Finger Off Trigger: Keep barrel pointed downrange/safe direction and index finger straight on the frame.
• 2. Unload & Lock Open: Remove magazine, lock slide/action open, and visually & physically inspect the chamber.
• 3. Load & Chamber Dummy Round: Load the provided inert dummy round, chamber safely, then eject and re-verify clear.
• 4. Apply DOJ Locking Device: Install the CA DOJ-approved cable lock cleanly through the open action.
