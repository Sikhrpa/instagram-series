Celebrating the Parkash Purab of Sri Guru Ram Das Ji (Oct 9 — founder of Sri Amritsar and embodiment of selfless Seva) and Gurgaddi Diwas of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji (Oct 20 — declared our Eternal Guru in 1708).

• Sri Guru Ram Das Ji (Oct 9): Taught boundless humility, community hospitality, and the sacred spirit of Amritsar.
• Gurgaddi Diwas (Oct 20 • 1708): Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji bestowed eternal Guruship upon Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji at Nanded.
• Living by Gurbani: Seeking wisdom, ethical restraint, and compassionate Seva in every corner of California.
