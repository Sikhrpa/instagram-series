Why Counterfeit Tourniquets Fail — Carry CoTCCC Approved Only

ਨਕਲੀ ਟੂਰਨੀਕੇਟ ਐਮਰਜੈਂਸੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਟੁੱਟ ਸਕਦੇ ਹਨ — ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਅਸਲੀ CoTCCC ਮਾਨਤਾ-ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤ ਵਰਤੋ

Generic look-alike tourniquets often bend or snap their windlass rod under real arterial pressure. Inspect your range bag IFAK today.

• Authentic CoTCCC Brands: Choose proven brands: CAT Gen 7 (North American Rescue), SOFTT-W, or SAM XT.
• Stage Out of Plastic Wrapper: Remove factory shrink-wrap before packing so it deploys one-handed in seconds.
• Inspect Windlass & Time Strap: Verify rigid composite/aluminum windlass, sharp stitching, and intact white time strap.
• Take Hands-On Training: Enroll in an accredited ACS Stop the Bleed® or Red Cross first-aid course.
