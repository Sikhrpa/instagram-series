First Time at a Public Range? Know These 5 Range Commands

ਪਹਿਲੀ ਵਾਰ ਸ਼ੂਟਿੰਗ ਰੇਂਜ 'ਤੇ ਜਾ ਰਹੇ ਹੋ? ਇਹ ੫ ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ ਰੇਂਜ ਕਮਾਂਡਾਂ ਯਾਦ ਰੱਖੋ

Public shooting ranges run on strict Range Safety Officer (RSO) commands. Knowing them beforehand keeps you calm, respectful, and 100% safe.

• 1. 'CEASE FIRE!': Stop immediately, finger off trigger, unload, lock action open, step behind the yellow/red line.
• 2. 'RANGE IS COLD': ZERO handling of firearms on the bench while anyone is downrange changing targets.
• 3. 'RANGE IS HOT': Eyes and ears ON before approaching the bench; safe firing may begin downrange.
• 4. Case & Uncase at Firing Line Only: Never uncase behind the line — muzzle must point strictly downrange when opening any bag.
