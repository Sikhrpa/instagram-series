De-Escalation First: California Brandishing Law (PC § 417) & Sikh Ethics

ਗੁੱਸੇ ਜਾਂ ਝਗੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਦੇ ਵੀ ਹਥਿਆਰ ਨਾ ਦਿਖਾਓ — ਸ਼ਾਂਤੀ ਅਤੇ ਕਾਨੂੰਨੀ ਅਨੁਸ਼ਾਸਨ ਸਭ ਤੋਂ ਉੱਪਰ

Both California law (PC § 417 & CALCRIM 3470) and the Zafarnama teach the exact same truth: a firearm is NEVER for intimidation, road rage, or personal arguments.

• PC § 417 Brandishing Crime: Displaying or drawing a firearm in a rude, angry, or threatening manner is a serious criminal offense.
• Road Rage & Ego Traps: Walk away, stay inside locked doors, and call 911 — winning an argument is never worth your freedom.
• Zafarnama Ethic of Last Resort: Force is ethically and legally justified ONLY when facing imminent deadly threat after all peaceful avenues fail.
