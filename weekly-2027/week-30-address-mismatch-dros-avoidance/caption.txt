Moved Recently? Fix Your CA DMV Address Before Visiting a Dealer

ਨਵੇਂ ਘਰ ਸ਼ਿਫਟ ਹੋਏ ਹੋ? ਡੀਲਰ ਕੋਲ ਜਾਣ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ DMV ਪਤਾ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਅਪਡੇਟ ਕਰੋ

An address mismatch between your California Driver's License, DMV record, and handgun Proof of Residency is one of the fastest ways to get delayed or rejected at the counter.

• Update CA DMV Online First: Submit your official change of address in the DMV database well ahead of time.
• Bring Updated Vehicle Registration: A current DMV registration card showing your new street address satisfies residency proof.
• Verify Every Character on DROS: Double-check apartment numbers, St vs. Ave, and middle initials before signing.
• Run Our 5-Item Readiness Audit: Check all paperwork free at sikhrpa.org/buyer-guide.html.
