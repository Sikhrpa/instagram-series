Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!

ਸ਼ਹੀਦਾਂ ਦੇ ਸਿਰਤਾਜ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਅਰਜਨ ਦੇਵ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਲਾਸਾਨੀ ਸ਼ਹਾਦਤ ਨੂੰ ਕੋਟਿ-ਕੋਟਿ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮ

Remembering the supreme peaceful sacrifice of the Fifth Guru (1606) with humble Chhabeel Seva, alongside the Parkash Purab of Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji (Jun 14), who established Miri-Piri.

• Tera Kiya Meetha Lage: Guru Arjan Dev Ji endured unimaginable torture with serene spiritual composure and love for all.
• Chhabeel & Cool Water Seva: Serving sweet cool water to neighbors honors Guru Ji's compassion during the summer heat.
• Foundation of Miri-Piri (1606): Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji united spiritual grace (Piri) with the civic duty to shield the oppressed (Miri).

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).
