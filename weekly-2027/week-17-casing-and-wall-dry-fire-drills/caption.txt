Two Proven Dry-Fire Drills to Fix Trigger Flinch at Home

ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਡਰਾਈ-ਫਾਇਰ ਅਭਿਆਸ — ਕਾਰਤੂਸ ਡ੍ਰਿਲ ਅਤੇ ੨-ਇੰਚ ਕੰਧ ਡ੍ਰਿਲ

Build rock-steady trigger control without firing a single live round — always starting with our strict 4-point unloaded safety verification.

• Mandatory Safety First: Unload, lock slide open, visually & physically verify clear, ZERO live ammo in the room.
• Drill 1: Spent Casing / Coin Drill: Balance a casing behind the front sight; aim for 10 smooth breaks without dropping it.
• Drill 2: The 2-Inch Wall Drill: Stand 2 inches from a blank wall and focus 100% on keeping the front sight completely still.
• 10 Minutes Daily: Short, deliberate, zero-flinch dry-fire sessions outperform rushing boxes of ammo.
