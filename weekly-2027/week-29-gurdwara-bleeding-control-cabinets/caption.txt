Does Your Gurdwara Have a Wall-Mounted Bleeding Control Kit?

ਗੁਰਦੁਆਰਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਐਮਰਜੈਂਸੀ ਬਲੀਡਿੰਗ ਕੰਟਰੋਲ ਕਿੱਟ ਅਤੇ ਸੇਵਾਦਾਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਸਿਖਲਾਈ

Just like AEDs and fire extinguishers, public-access Stop the Bleed wall cabinets save lives during kitchen accidents, parking-lot injuries, or community emergencies.

• Pair Next to Your AED: Mount a clearly labeled Bleeding Control station beside the Gurdwara's AED unit.
• Train Langar & Security Sevadaars: Equip volunteers with hands-on tourniquet, wound-packing, and 911 dispatch readiness.
• Bilingual Emergency Cards: Post English & Punjabi step-by-step reference guides inside the cabinet.
• Free SikhRPA Consultation: Email president@sikhrpa.org — SikhRPA assists California Gurdwara committees at zero cost.

Explore 100% free English & Gurmukhi safety tools anytime at sikhrpa.org/trauma-safety.html (Link in bio).
