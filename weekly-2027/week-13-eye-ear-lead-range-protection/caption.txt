Range PPE Essentials: Eyes, Double Ears & Lead Hygiene

ਰੇਂਜ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ — ਅੱਖਾਂ (ANSI Z87.1), ਕੰਨਾਂ ਦੀ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਅਤੇ ਲੈੱਡ (Lead) ਤੋਂ ਸਫ਼ਾਈ

Hearing damage is permanent and lead residue transfers to cars and homes. Build a rock-solid personal protection routine for every range trip.

• ANSI Z87.1 Eye Protection: Always wear impact-rated wrap-around eyewear to shield against brass and debris.
• Double Up Hearing Protection: Pair foam earplugs under 30+ dB earmuffs — especially at indoor ranges.
• Cold Water + Lead-Removing Soap: Wash hands and face with COLD water (hot water opens pores) before eating or driving.
• Dedicated Range Shoes & Clothing: Change shoes before entering homes with crawling toddlers or infants.
