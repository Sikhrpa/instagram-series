The 70 / 30 Grip Rule: Eliminating Sympathetic Squeeze

ਸਹੀ ਗ੍ਰਿੱਪ ਤਕਨੀਕ — 70% ਸਪੋਰਟ ਹੱਥ ਦਾ ਦਬਾਅ ਅਤੇ 30% ਮੁੱਖ ਹੱਥ ਦਾ ਸੰਤੁਲਨ

Your dominant hand shouldn't death-grip the pistol. Transferring 70% of clamping pressure to your support hand frees your trigger finger to press straight back.

• 70% Support Hand Clamp: Fill the exposed grip panel firmly with your support palm and lock your wrist forward.
• 30% Dominant Hand: Hold like a firm handshake so your index finger hinges independently.
• Empty-Hand Diagnostic: Curl your trigger finger empty-handed — watch if your pinky twitches inward.
• Free Interactive Analyzer: Diagnose target patterns right or left-handed at sikhrpa.org/shot-diagnosis.html.
