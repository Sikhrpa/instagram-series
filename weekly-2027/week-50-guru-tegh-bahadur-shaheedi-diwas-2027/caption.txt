Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!

ਹਿੰਦ ਦੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਤੇਗ਼ ਬਹਾਦਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਲਾਸਾਨੀ ਸ਼ਹਾਦਤ ਨੂੰ ਕੋਟਿ-ਕੋਟਿ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮ

Bowing in solemn tribute to the Ninth Guru, who gave His life in Delhi (1675) to defend freedom of conscience and the right of every human being to live without tyranny.

• Defender of All Humanity: Stood fearlessly for the religious freedom and dignity of people beyond His own faith.
• Bhai Mati Das, Bhai Sati Das & Bhai Dayala Ji: Remembering the devoted Sikhs who stood unwavering beside Guru Ji.
• Living Without Fear or Hatred: Bhay Kahu Ko Det Neh, Neh Bhay Manat Aan — neither intimidate nor submit to tyranny.

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).

ਸਰਬੱਤ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ • ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਰਹੋ, ਜ਼ਿੰਮੇਵਾਰ ਬਣੋ! — Stay Safe, Be Responsible!
