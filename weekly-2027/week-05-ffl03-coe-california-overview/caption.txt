Understanding California's FFL03 + COE Credential Process

ਕੈਲੀਫੋਰਨੀਆ FFL03 (ਫੈਡਰਲ ਕਲੈਕਟਰ ਲਾਇਸੈਂਸ) ਅਤੇ COE ਪ੍ਰਕਿਰਿਆ ਦੀ ਕਾਨੂੰਨੀ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ

An educational overview of the federal Curio & Relic Collector's License (ATF Form 7 CR) paired with California's DOJ Certificate of Eligibility (CFARS).

• Phase 1 — ATF Form 7 CR (FFL03): Individual federal collector license ($30 / 3-year term) + informational copy to local CLEO.
• Phase 2 — CA DOJ COE (CFARS): Apply online via CFARS and complete authorized California Live Scan fingerprinting.
• Annual Renewal & Record-Keeping: Maintain an A&D bound book for any C&R items and renew your CA COE annually.
• Keep Address Current: Notify both ATF and CA DOJ promptly if you move so credentials always match.
