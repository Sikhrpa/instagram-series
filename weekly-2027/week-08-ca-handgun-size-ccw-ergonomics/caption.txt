Understanding Handgun Ergonomics: Grip Height, Length & Fit

ਹੈਂਡਗੰਨ ਸਾਈਜ਼ ਅਤੇ ਗ੍ਰਿੱਪ ਫਿੱਟ ਦੀ ਵਿਗਿਆਨਕ ਤੁਲਨਾ — 1:1 ਸਕੇਲ ਗਾਈਡ

A firearm that fits your hand promotes safer trigger reach and better recoil control. Compare California Roster dimensions 1:1 at sikhrpa.org/handgun-compare.html.

• Grip Height (Printing Factor): Grip height — not barrel length — is the #1 dimensional factor in concealed-carry silhouette.
• Slide Length & Keel Stability: Slightly longer slides improve sight radius and holster balance along the belt line.
• Trigger Reach & Control: Ensure the center pad of your index finger reaches the trigger cleanly without breaking grip.
• 1:1 True-Scale Overlay Tool: Compare CA Roster dimensions side-by-side free at sikhrpa.org/handgun-compare.html.
