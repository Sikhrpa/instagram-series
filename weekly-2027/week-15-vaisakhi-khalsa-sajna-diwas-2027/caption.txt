Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!

ਵਿਸਾਖੀ ਅਤੇ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ ਸਾਜਨਾ ਦਿਵਸ ਦੀਆਂ ਸਮੂਹ ਸੰਗਤ ਨੂੰ ਲੱਖ-ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ!

On Vaisakhi 1699 at Sri Anandpur Sahib, Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji established the Khalsa Panth — uniting equality, fearless integrity, and selfless defense of human dignity.

• Equality & Courage (1699): The Panj Pyare stood for truth across every background, caste, and region.
• Moral Covenant of Duty: Strength is forever bound to humility, compassion, and protecting the defenseless.
• Sarbat da Bhala: Wishing every family and Nagar Kirtan Sangat across California a joyful, safe Vaisakhi!

Learn more about Sikh heritage, Miri-Piri, and our free bilingual safety resources at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).
