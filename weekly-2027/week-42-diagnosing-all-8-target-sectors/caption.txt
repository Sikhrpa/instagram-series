Reading Your Target: What High-Right, 6:00 Low & 9:00 Left Mean

ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨੇਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਗਲਤੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਪਛਾਣ — 8-ਸੈਕਟਰ ਟਾਰਗੈੱਟ ਡਾਇਲ ਗਾਈਡ

While 7:00–8:00 Low-Left is the #1 pattern, every clock sector on your target reveals a specific biomechanical habit you can fix cleanly.

• 6:00 Straight Low: Breaking both wrists downward or dropping your head right before ignition.
• 9:00 Straight Left (Too Little Finger): Pressing with only the extreme fingertip pushes the frame sideways like a wedge.
• 1:00–2:00 High-Right (Heeling): Shoving the heel of your palm forward into the backstrap as the shot breaks.
• Interactive Right & Left-Handed Wheel: Tap your exact impact zone free at sikhrpa.org/shot-diagnosis.html.
