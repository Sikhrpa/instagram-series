Celebrating Sikh Heritage Month Through Community Safety

ਅਪ੍ਰੈਲ ਸਿੱਖ ਵਿਰਾਸਤ ਮਹੀਨਾ — ਸਿੱਖਿਆ, ਸੇਵਾ ਅਤੇ ਪਰਿਵਾਰਕ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ

Every April, California celebrates Sikh Heritage Month — honoring Sikh contributions to civic life, service (Seva), and responsible community leadership.

• Seva Through Public Safety: SikhRPA serves California families of all backgrounds with 100% free bilingual safety tools.
• Honoring Miri-Piri: Balancing spiritual humility with civic stewardship and respect for the law.
• Share Bilingual Guides: Send sikhrpa.org to parents, elders, and new California residents this month.

Explore 100% free English & Gurmukhi safety tools anytime at sikhrpa.org/heritage.html (Link in bio).
