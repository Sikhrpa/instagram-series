The Ball & Dummy Drill: Exposing Recoil Anticipation Instantly

ਬਾਲ ਐਂਡ ਡੰਮੀ ਡ੍ਰਿਲ — ਰੀਕੋਇਲ ਦੇ ਝਟਕੇ ਨੂੰ ਤੁਰੰਤ ਪਛਾਣਨ ਦਾ ਸਭ ਤੋਂ ਵਧੀਆ ਤਰੀਕਾ

Think you aren't flinching? Have a trusted range partner mix one inert snap cap randomly into your magazine — your sights will tell the exact truth.

• How to Set It Up Safely: A range partner loads 4 live rounds + 1 bright inert snap cap in a secret order.
• Watch the Front Sight on 'Click': When you press on the dummy round, any downward dip exposes subconscious flinching.
• Correct Immediately: Relax your dominant hand to 30%, clamp 70% with support hand, and press straight back.
• Target Standard: Zero muzzle dip when the striker clicks on the inert dummy round.
