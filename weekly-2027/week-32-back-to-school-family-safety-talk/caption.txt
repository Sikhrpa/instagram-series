Back-to-School Safety: Asking About Safe Storage Before Playdates

ਸਕੂਲ ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਹੋਣ 'ਤੇ ਮਾਪਿਆਂ ਲਈ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ — ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਸਭ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ

Just as parents ask about food allergies or swimming pools before a playdate, having calm, respectful conversations about locked home storage keeps every child safe.

• Normalize Calm Safety Questions: Asking whether firearms are locked in a safe protects kids without judgment.
• Review the 4 Kid Rules Together: Practice STOP • Don't Touch • Leave Area • Tell an Adult before school starts.
• Audit After-School Rooms: Verify safes and lockboxes are latched whenever teens or friends visit after school.
• Free Bilingual Family Leaflets: Download printable guides anytime at sikhrpa.org.
