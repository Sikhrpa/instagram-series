Stop Slapping the Trigger: Master Slack Take-Up & Reset

ਟਰਿੱਗਰ ਨੂੰ ਕਦੇ ਝਟਕੇ ਨਾਲ ਨਾ ਛੱਡੋ — ਰੀਸੈੱਟ ਦੀ 'ਟਿਕ' ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਤੱਕ ਹੀ ਉਂਗਲ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਕਰੋ

Slapping the trigger from full forward travel disturbs sight alignment. Riding the tactile reset cuts trigger travel by up to 60% for smoother follow-through.

• Take Up Initial Slack: Smoothly press through light pre-travel until you feel the crisp 'wall' before firing.
• Press Straight Through the Wall: Add smooth rearward pressure using the center pad of your index finger.
• Hold, Then Release to Reset: After firing, ease forward ONLY until you feel/hear the tactile reset click.
• Practice Safely Dry-Fire: Review all 4 neuromuscular drills at sikhrpa.org/shot-diagnosis.html.
