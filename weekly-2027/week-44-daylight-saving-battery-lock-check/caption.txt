Clocks Fall Back This Week: Check Optic & Safe Batteries Too!

ਸਮਾਂ ਬਦਲਣ (Fall Back) ਦੇ ਨਾਲ ਆਪਣੀ ਸੇਫ਼ ਅਤੇ ਓਪਟਿਕ ਦੀਆਂ ਬੈਟਰੀਆਂ ਵੀ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਬਦਲੋ

Tie your safety gear check to Daylight Saving Time every November: fresh smoke-alarm batteries, fresh lockbox batteries, and fresh optic batteries.

• Electronic Lockboxes & Safes: Swap batteries before low-voltage warnings lock you out or cause failed reads.
• Red Dot Optics: Install a fresh quality coin cell (e.g., CR2032/CR1632) and verify torque & zero.
• Weapon/Handheld Flashlights: Test lumen output & pack spare batteries in your home emergency kit.
• IFAK Expiration Check: Check hemostatic gauze and chest seal integrity once a year.
