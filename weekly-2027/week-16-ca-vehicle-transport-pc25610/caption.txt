How to Legally Transport Firearms in Your Vehicle in California

ਕੈਲੀਫੋਰਨੀਆ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਾਰ ਰਾਹੀਂ ਹਥਿਆਰ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਅਤੇ ਕਾਨੂੰਨੀ ਤੌਰ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਜਾਣ ਦੇ ਨਿਯਮ

Heading to the range? One mistake in your car — like using the glovebox or leaving a loaded magazine inserted — can violate California law.

• 100% Unloaded: Verify chamber empty and no loaded magazine inserted in the firearm.
• Locked Container or Trunk: Place handguns in a fully enclosed locked container or inside a separate locked trunk.
• NEVER Glovebox or Console: CA PC § 16850 explicitly excludes utility compartments, gloveboxes, and center consoles.
• Direct Destination Rule: Keep transport direct to/from lawful destinations (range, home, FFL) and never leave unattended.
