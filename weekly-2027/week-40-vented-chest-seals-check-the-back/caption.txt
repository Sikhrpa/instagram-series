Chest Seals 101: Always Roll & Check the Back for an Exit Wound

ਛਾਤੀ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ 'ਤੇ ਵੈਂਟਡ ਚੈਸਟ ਸੀਲ — ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਦੀ ਪਿੱਠ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਚੈੱਕ ਕਰੋ!

Torso wounds between the neck and navel can trap air inside the chest cavity. That's why every range IFAK carries a twin-pack of vented chest seals.

• Wipe Blood & Sweat First: Dry the skin around the wound so the hydrogel adhesive seals securely.
• Apply Vented Seal on Exhale: One-way vents let trapped air escape while blocking outside air from entering.
• Always Check the Back!: Roll the patient carefully and physically check for an exit wound — seal it with Seal #2.
• Educational Awareness Only: Always call 911 first & pursue accredited ACS Stop the Bleed® training.
