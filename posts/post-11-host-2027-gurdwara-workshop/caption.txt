Planning your Gurdwara Sahib or Sikh community center's 2027 educational calendar? Partner with SikhRPA to host a 100% FREE bilingual Safety & Safe Storage Workshop!

What SikhRPA provides at zero cost to your Gurdwara or Sangat:
• Certified bilingual instructors presenting in Punjabi & English
• Practical California Safe Storage education (CA Penal Code § 25100) & child safety rules
• Welcoming, classroom-only environment open to families, women (Bibian), elders & absolute beginners
• Optional Stop the Bleed / Gurdwara bleeding-control cabinet & Sevadaar readiness briefing
• FREE CA DOJ-approved cable safety locks & printed bilingual safety handbooks for every attending family
