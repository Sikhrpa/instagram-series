During Shaheedi Week (Poh / late December), we bow our heads in solemn remembrance of the Chaar Sahibzaade — Baba Ajit Singh Ji, Baba Jujhar Singh Ji, Baba Zorawar Singh Ji, and Baba Fateh Singh Ji — alongside Mata Gujri Ji and the devoted Sikhs of Chamkaur Sahib and Sirhind.

Their sacrifice illuminates the deepest truth of Sikh martial heritage: strength is never rooted in aggression, vanity, or dominance. It is bound to spiritual humility (Piri), moral restraint (Miri), and unwavering protection of human dignity.

In the Zafarnama (Verse 22), Guru Gobind Singh Ji articulated the ethic that guides our educational mission to this day:
ਚੁ ਕਾਰ ਅਜ਼ ਹਮਹ ਹੀਲਤੇ ਦਰ ਗੁਜ਼ਸ਼ਤ ॥
ਹਲਾਲ ਅਸਤ ਬੁਰਦਨ ਬ ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ੀਰ ਦਸਤ ॥੨੨॥
"When all other peaceful avenues have been exhausted and every remedy has failed, only then is it righteous to take up the sword."

Today, honoring that sacred covenant of Shastar reverence means exercising total discipline: de-escalation first, strict obedience to the law, safe home storage to protect every child, and humble service (Seva) to our Sangat and neighbors.
