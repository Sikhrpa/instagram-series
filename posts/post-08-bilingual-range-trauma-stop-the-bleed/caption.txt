--- CAPTION (READY TO PASTE) ---

If you carry safety gear to the range, carry the training and supplies to stop life-threatening bleeding too. Severe arterial bleeding can become fatal in minutes — long before an ambulance reaches a remote outdoor range.

Swipe through SikhRPA's Bilingual Range Trauma & Stop the Bleed Awareness Guide:
• First 60 Seconds: Call 'CEASE FIRE!' & Dial 911 → Find Bleeding Source → Compress / Tourniquet
• 4 Mandatory Tourniquet Rules in English & Gurmukhi (ਟੂਰਨੀਕੇਟ ਲਗਾਉਣ ਦੇ ੪ ਲਾਜ਼ਮੀ ਨਿਯਮ): 2–3" above wound (never on a joint), pull strap max tight, twist windlass until bleeding stops, write time (T: HH:MM) & NEVER loosen
• Junctional Wound Packing, Vented Chest Seals (check the back for exit wounds!), & Hypothermia Prevention
• 6-Item Range Bag IFAK Checklist (use only authentic CoTCCC-approved tourniquets like CAT Gen 7, SOFTT-W, or SAM XT)
• Verbatim Remote Range 911 Dispatch Script with GPS coordinates & highway gate escort

Read the full bilingual guide & use the interactive IFAK Auditor and GPS 911 Script Generator at sikhrpa.org/trauma-safety.html (Link in bio).

Medical Disclaimer: Provided strictly for general safety awareness and does not constitute medical advice. Always dial 911 in an emergency and pursue formal hands-on training through the American College of Surgeons Stop the Bleed® program or the American Red Cross.
