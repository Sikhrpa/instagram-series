Hosting family, friends, and visiting children this holiday season? Take 3 minutes to audit your home firearm storage BEFORE guests arrive.

Under California Penal Code § 25100 and § 25140, criminal storage of an unsecured firearm where a minor under 18 or prohibited person gains access and causes harm is a serious felony. Hiding a firearm on a high closet shelf or inside a nightstand drawer is NOT a legal defense — and never keeps curious children safe.
