Meet the Sikh Rifle and Pistol Association (SikhRPA) — an IRS-recognized California 501(c)(3) nonprofit public benefit corporation dedicated to firearm safety education, safe home storage, statutory compliance, and emergency trauma readiness.

Everything we publish at sikhrpa.org is 100% free and accessible in both English and Gurmukhi:
• Free 30-Question CA Firearm Safety Certificate (FSC) Practice Exam
• California First-Time Compliance Roadmap (REAL ID, Proof of Residency & DROS)
• Bilingual Range Trauma & Stop the Bleed First-Aid Guide
• Interactive Pistol Shot Diagnostic & Optics Zeroing Calculators
• Daily California Legal & Regulatory Watch Archive
• Free Gurdwara Safe Storage Workshops & CA DOJ-Approved Cable Locks

This #GivingTuesday, every tax-deductible contribution directly funds printed bilingual safety guides, free CA DOJ-approved cable locks for families, and community safety clinics across California.

• $20 = 1 CA DOJ-approved cable safety lock for a local family
• $50 = 100 pocket-sized English & Gurmukhi safety manuals
• $150 = Certified eye & ear protection kits for 10 beginner students
• $1,000 = Sponsors a full bilingual Gurdwara safety workshop
