California has some of the most specific statutory documentation rules in the nation — and simple paperwork oversights are the #1 reason lawful first-time applicants get turned away at the counter.

Swipe through SikhRPA's 5-Step California Compliance Roadmap to prepare every document properly:
1. Primary ID (CA PC § 26845): Check the top-right corner of your CA Driver's License! If it says 'FEDERAL LIMITS APPLY' instead of displaying the REAL ID Golden Bear & Star, you MUST bring an unexpired U.S. Passport, certified U.S. Birth Certificate, or valid Green Card (Form I-551).
2. Handgun Proof of Residency (11 CCR § 4045.1): Current DMV registration, municipal utility bill (≤90 days), signed residential lease, or property deed matching your ID address. Cell phone bills, bank statements, and internet/cable bills are strictly rejected.
3. Firearm Safety Certificate (CA PC § 31615): 30-question exam (75% passing, $25 fee, valid 5 years).
4. Exact 10-Day (240-Hour) DROS Wait (CA PC § 26815 & § 28220): Counted to the exact second, with a hard 30-day pickup expiration window.
5. Delivery & Legal Transport (CA PC § 26850 & § 25610): Complete the Safe Handling Demonstration, verify a DOJ-approved lock/safe affidavit, and transport unloaded inside a locked container or trunk.
