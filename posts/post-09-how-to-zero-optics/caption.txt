Stop burning ammunition chasing single bullet holes on paper! Zeroing a pistol red dot or rifle scope is pure geometry once you understand four simple principles.

Swipe through SikhRPA's visual technical guide to zeroing optics accurately:
• 5-Shot Group Centroid Triangulation: Never adjust for one shot — fire a rested 3–5 shot group, find the Mean Point of Impact (MPI), and dial once.
• MOA vs. MIL (MRAD): 1 MOA ≈ 1.047" at 100 yds (1/4, 1/2, or 1 MOA clicks) vs. 1 MIL = 3.60" at 100 yds (0.1 MIL = 0.36" per click). Golden Rule: turret arrows ('UP' / 'R') move your bullet impact (POI)!
• 25 / 50 / 100-Yard Click Scaling: Closer targets mean smaller inch-per-click shifts — so 25 yards requires 4× as many clicks per inch as 100 yards.
• Height-Over-Bore Mechanical Offset: Because an optic sits ~2.6" above a carbine bore (~0.8" on pistols), close 3–7 yard shots naturally hit 1.5"–2.5" below your dot.
