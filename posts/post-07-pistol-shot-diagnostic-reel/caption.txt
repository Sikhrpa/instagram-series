Are your pistol shots consistently landing low and to the left (7:00–8:00 o'clock)? Nearly 85% of right-handed shooters experience this exact pattern — and it is almost never your sights.

What's really happening biomechanically:
1. Sympathetic Grip Squeeze: As your index finger presses the trigger, your bottom three fingers (middle, ring, pinky) clench involuntarily, pulling the muzzle downward and inward.
2. Recoil Anticipation: Pre-emptively pushing the wrist down right before the shot breaks.
3. Trigger Finger Placement: Hooking too deep into the 1st joint crease drags the frame left; use the center pad sweet spot and hinge straight rearward.

The Fix — The 70/30 Grip Rule:
Apply ~70% clamping pressure with your support hand and keep your dominant hand relaxed (~30%) so your trigger finger moves independently.

Dry-Fire Safety Checklist (BEFORE every drill):
✓ Visually & physically verify chamber & magwell are 100% clear
✓ Remove ALL live ammunition from the room
✓ Point strictly at a safe, solid backstop
